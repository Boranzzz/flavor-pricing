  3. Formula
    Price on flavors, pairs, and fixed effects.

    log P = α + β′F + θ′(F × F) + γ′X + δ + λ

    1. P
      Price, standardized by currency, bottle size, and period.
    2. F
      Flavor indicators. Peaty, Fruity, Smoky, Medicinal, Floral, Spicy.
    3. θ
      Extra premium when two flavors appear together, beyond the two main effects.
    4. X
      Age, ABV, cask, and other bottle traits.
    5. δ, λ
      Distillery and year.

  5. Draft
    Which pairs are priced beyond their parts.

    The market may price Peaty × Fruity, or Smoky × Medicinal, above the sum of the two flavors. That extra gap is the object, not the flavor itself.

    Pairs will be chosen by how often they occur and by whether the combination means something, not by searching every interaction for a significant one.

  6. Note
    A review date is not a sale.

    Whisky Advocate dates a review, not a transaction. A historical premium needs a price observed in the same period as the flavor description.

    Where the two dates differ, keep both, and do not treat the review date as the sale date.
